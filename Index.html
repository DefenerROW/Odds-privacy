Odds Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026 · Contact: benson.mitch@gmail.com

Odds is an informational app that answers probability-style questions using base rates and public prediction-market data. It is not a sportsbook and does not place bets.

What we collect

Odds does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or payment information inside the app.

Questions you type stay on your device for that session. We do not operate a user database of your searches.

Network requests

When you ask a question, the app may query public market APIs (including Polymarket and Kalshi) to fetch live prices. Those services may receive the search text needed to find a matching market, plus standard technical data (IP address, device/user-agent) as with any HTTPS request.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not show third-party advertising SDKs in the current version.
  • We do not use your questions to train a model on our servers (there is no Odds backend account system in this version).

Children

Odds is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
